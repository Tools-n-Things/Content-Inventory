List every piece of content in your program — what it is, how current it is, and how urgent it is. Your designer uses this to plan the work, so rough answers are fine. You don't need an account or any special tools.

How this works

Your work saves in this browser as you go. When you're done — or any time you want to send the designer an update — download your file and email it to them. If several trainers share a program, each of you works on your own pieces and sends your own file.
